The Wyrm Dungeon

WASD / arrows move · F choose · G back · hold F to speed up attacks · M sound
The Gloomies stole Mossbrook's Sunstone, and you. Break out of the cells, get past the Warden's prison, then decide: escape, or go deeper for the Sunstone.
In battle, press F as the marker hits the green for a GREAT hit, or to GUARD when a Gloomy swings at you.
In this demo, CPU squadmates stand in for players at the other cabinets in the server (up to 4).
